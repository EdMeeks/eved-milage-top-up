If you do not have any documents

If you have not received a V11 reminder, DVLA may not have your correct address.

If your address is wrong, you must change your address on your vehicle log book (V5C) .

If you have lost your V5C you can get a new vehicle log book (V5C) .

You can also find out how to tax your vehicle without a V11 reminder .
