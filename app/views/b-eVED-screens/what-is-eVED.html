Electric Vehicle Excise Duty (eVED)

From , your vehicle will be charged Electric Vehicle Excise Duty (eVED).

What is eVED?

eVED is a mileage based road tax for electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles. You will need to pay eVED and standard vehicle tax (VED).

Electric vehicles are charged 3p per mile and plug-in hybrids are charged 1.5p per mile.

What happens next

  • you’ll need to tell us your vehicle’s current mileage
  • you’ll then estimate how many miles you expect to drive over the next year
