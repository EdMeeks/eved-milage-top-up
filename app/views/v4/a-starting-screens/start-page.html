Pay for additional mileage of your electric car

This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg).

Use this service to buy and pay for additional mileage for your electric or plug-in hybrid car, whenever you need to purchase more miles.

Rates and costs

The cost depends on your car type:

  • fully electric cars: 3p per mile
  • plug-in hybrid cars (PHEVs): 1.5p per mile

Example costs

If you need to add 1,000 miles, the total cost will be:

  • £30.00 for a fully electric car
  • £15.00 for a plug-in hybrid car

You will see the exact final calculation before making a payment.

Vehicle tax Direct Debit payments

If you pay your vehicle tax and eVED by monthly Direct Debit, the 'Paid up to mileage' value may include miles covered by upcoming payments that you have not paid up to yet.

If you cancel your Direct Debit, we will recalculate your 'Paid up to mileage' based only on the payments we have successfully collected.

What to do if you exceed your paid up to mileage

If you drive more miles than you've paid up to, you'll have a mileage debt.

You can either:

  • buy additional miles to clear any mileage debt
  • leave the mileage debt until you next tax your vehicle. The cost of the mileage debt will be added to your next tax transaction, so you’ll pay more at that time

Before you start

You will need:

  • the car's registration number (number plate)
  • the log book (V5C) reference number
  • the current odometer (mileage) reading
  • a debit or credit card
If you opt in to providing an email, you will receive a confirmation email as soon as your payment is complete.
