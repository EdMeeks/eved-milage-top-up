Pay by Direct Debit

Direct Debit payments are taken on the first working day of each month.

Your first payment date may be different.

You cannot change the date your payments are taken.

Your Direct Debit will not renew automatically. You'll need to set up a new Direct Debit the next time you tax this vehicle.

If you tell us you've sold the vehicle, we'll cancel your Direct Debit.

! Warning If you do not keep up with your payments, your vehicle tax will be cancelled and we may take enforcement action
