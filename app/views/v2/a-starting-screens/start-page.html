Pay for additional mileage of your electric vehicle

This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg).

Use this service to buy and pay for additional mileage for your electric vehicle or plug-in hybrid if you have exceeded your vehicle's annual mileage allowance.

You can use this service to top up your mileage whenever you need to purchase more miles.

Rates and costs

The cost depends on your vehicle type:

  • fully electric vehicles: 3p per mile
  • plug-in hybrid vehicles (PHEVs): 1.5p per mile

Example costs

If you need to add 1,000 miles, the total cost will be:

  • £30.00 for a fully electric vehicle
  • 15.00 for a plug-in hybrid vehicle

You will see the exact final calculation before making a payment.

Before you start

You will need:

  • the vehicle registration number (number plate)
  • the vehicle log book (V5C) reference number
  • the current odometer (mileage) reading
  • a debit or credit card
You will receive a confirmation email as soon as your payment is complete.
