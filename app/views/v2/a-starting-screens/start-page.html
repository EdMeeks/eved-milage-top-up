Pay for additional mileage of your electric car

This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg).

Use this service to buy and pay for additional mileage for your electric car or plug-in hybrid car if you have exceeded the paid for mileage.

You can use this service to top up your mileage whenever you need to purchase more miles.

Rates and costs

The cost depends on your car type:

  • fully electric cars: 3p per mile
  • plug-in hybrid cars (PHEVs): 1.5p per mile

Example costs

If you need to add 1,000 miles, the total cost will be:

  • £30.00 for a fully electric car
  • £15.00 for a plug-in hybrid car

You will see the exact final calculation before making a payment.

Before you start

You will need:

  • the car's registration number (number plate)
  • the log book (V5C) reference number
  • the current odometer (mileage) reading
  • a debit or credit card
If you opt in to providing an email, you will receive a confirmation email as soon as your payment is complete.
