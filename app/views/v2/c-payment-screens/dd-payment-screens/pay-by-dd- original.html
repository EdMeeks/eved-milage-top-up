Pay by Direct Debit

Direct Debit payments will be taken on the first working day of the month. Your first payment date may be different. These dates cannot be changed.

Your Direct Debit payments will renew automatically unless you notify us of any changes. We are unable to change the date payments are collected. Failure to keep up these payments may result in you being liable for enforcement action and this payment option being removed from you in the future.

If you tell us you have sold the vehicle, we will then cancel your Direct Debit.

DVLA handles your personal data in accordance with road vehicle law and data protection laws. The law allows us to release your data to the police and other enforcement bodies. We also provide data to other parties where the law allows it. For further information about how we process your data, your rights and who to contact, see our privacy notice at www.gov.uk/dvla/privacy-policy .
