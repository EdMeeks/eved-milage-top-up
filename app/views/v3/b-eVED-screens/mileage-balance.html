{# Page Content: What 'paid for mileage' means #}

Paid for mileage is the mileage that has been paid for to date as the owner or keeper of the vehicle in the DVLA eVED service.

It reflects your registered history with the vehicle, not just the miles bought in your most recent transaction.

How it is calculated

The system calculates this total by recording your vehicle's odometer readings over time:

  1. The baseline: It starts with the mileage reading entered the very first time you added miles as the registered owner or keeper.
  2. The accumulation: Every subsequent mile purchased is added to this baseline to keep an accurate record of your total paid-for balance.
