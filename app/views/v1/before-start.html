What are we testing this round?

Round 1 User Research Objectives

1. Understand the 'happy path' for users in debit

  • Objective: Evaluate how easily and successfully users can complete the end-to-end journey when they have exceeded their estimated mileage.
  • Hypothesis: Users can navigate the system without friction, even when their account is in deficit.

2. Assess the comprehension of data presentation

  • Objective: Understand how clearly users interpret their mileage data, account balances, and usage metrics on the dashboard.
  • Hypothesis: The layout and language allow users to accurately assess their current usage at a glance.

3. Explore user behavior around purchasing additional miles

  • Objective: Determine if, how, and when users want to top up or add more miles to their account when they are in debit.
  • Hypothesis: Users expect an immediate, intuitive way to clear their deficit and add a buffer for future travel.

4. Gauge overall user perception of value and service

  • Objective: Gather feedback on how users perceive the overall service, the fairness of the pricing structure, and any unexpected costs.
  • Hypothesis: Transparency in pricing builds trust and reduces anxiety about variable monthly costs.
