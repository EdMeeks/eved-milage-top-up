Pay for additional mileage of your electric vehicle

This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg).

Use this service to pay for additional mileage for your electric vehicle if you have exceeded your vehicle's annual mileage allowance.

You can use this service if you have a UK vehicle registration and need to pay for mileage above your annual allowance.

What you need

  • the registration number (number plate)
  • your vehicle registration V5c certificate
  • the current mileage reading
  • a debit or credit card

Cost

The cost depends on your vehicle type and how much you've exceeded your allowance. You'll see the exact amount before you pay.

You'll get a confirmation email when your payment is complete.
